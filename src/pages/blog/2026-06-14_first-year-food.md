My #1 activity with friends is getting food and bubble tea together, because obviously we always deserve a break from studying. Here are some of my favourites from my first year in Toronto! (I had planned to write this blog post a lot earlier, whoops...)

I use [Beli](https://beliapp.com/) to keep track of my restaurant & café rankings!

# category I: food, because we need to eat

1. **A Szechuan Restaurant <span lang="zh" class="text-lg">一家川菜馆</span>** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pEYQ4f7vzvWjQ4d8A), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/aszechuanrestaurant/))

AMAZING -- can't go wrong with anything on the menu. I especially love the 花蛤 (clams) here and this restaurant is very much parent-approved.

A bit pricey for each indiviudal dish, but the portion sizes are really really big, so once you count how many meals you can eat with the leftovers, it's a good deal.

2. **Bao House** (Google Maps: [Dundas](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HJNP4kiFB116tdPw9) / [Yonge & Wellesley](https://maps.app.goo.gl/eXHS2TbwThZieiGt7), [Website](https://www.bao-house.ca/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/baohouseto/))

Very good price for comfort foods. My faves: pork & cabbage pan-fried dumplings, braised beef noodle soup, and any 小笼包 (xiaolongbao / soup dumplings).

The Dundas location is bigger and has more drink options -- the jasmine milk tea was quite good and only ~$3!

3. **Grandma Kitchen <span lang="zh" class="text-lg">阿婆私房菜</span>** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9vwSjmPn8XZm9Abh9), [Website](https://grandmakitchens.ca/))

Came here multiple times (with friends' parents too, who had apparently heard about this from 小红书, so this is many-parent-approved). I like the fried rice, 干煸四季豆 (Sichuan dry-fried green beans), and eggplant!

4. **Mom's Pan-Fried Bun <span lang="zh" class="text-lg">老娘水煎包</span>** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/T3YitDBuH9RVpeMr8), [Website](https://momspanfriedbun.com/))

Super good prices!! I liked the mango sago a lot and the 凉皮 (Shaanxi cold noodles) especially.

5. **Fudao Noodle House <span lang="zh" class="text-lg">福稻过桥米线</span>** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ACB6tSsqDDcggbXG8), [Website](https://fudao.ca/))

Huuuuge portion sizes for a good price. Nice for takeout since it's individually portioned (not family-style). I got the braised pork and this was also the first time I actually enjoyed fatty meat.

# category II: bubble tea, because we enjoy being happy

1. **Molly Tea** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/F1KbEQ5UmRJoxvBB6), [Website](https://mollyteaca.com/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mollytea_canada/))

Insanely fresh tea flavour -- come here for milk teas, not toppings, except the snowy whipped cream (yummm). I swear the thin straws do something. Also, my favourite and absolutely underrated drink: osmanthus green milk tea (桂花龙井).

2. **Hero Tea** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/r46gBHQJGFeY2Fvk7), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/heroteacanada/))

Similar fresh milk tea style to Molly Tea but a bit more pricey, and I would say just a tiny bit weaker tea flavour. Still the best milk tea available in Ottawa. My favourite (as expected): snow-topped osmanthus blossom (I love that it comes with the whipped cream).

3. **Chatime** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Jt9PJUyUFcZHufYH6), [Website](https://chatime.ca/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/chatimecanada/))

Came here WAY too many times, but Chatime rewards are actually pretty decent. It's definitely a lower-tier kind of brand than the first two I mentioned, but the tea taste is still quite strong. Importantly for me, Chatime tapioca pearls are always good (surprisingly harder to find than I thought). I also bought too many of the plushie keychain blind boxes (and got really unlucky) because I had friends who wanted them lol...

4. **Heytea** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DtaiYxTBdyEyCS6T8), [Website](https://www.heytea.com/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/heytea.ca/))

The new Crisp Grape Boom drink that we tried was really good, although I guess it wouldn't really fall under the category of tea?

5. **Baroness** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/EJg5Jg3ppJYPtMpPA), [Website](https://www.baronesscanada.com/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/baronessontario/))

Local business with a super cute mural and nice atmosphere! :D I like their menu -- it has a lot of cool drinks, and the peach oolong tea was decent. They have some really good deals on certain days.

# category III: matcha, because we love drinking grass

I'm tracking prices in this category...

1. **Lost in Tokyo Café & Bar** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ge4ooNsfHeAgRoGq9), [Website](https://lostintokyo.online/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lostintokyo.parfait))

Strawberry matcha latte: **$7.90** before tax _(but you get $1 off if you bring your own cup!)_. The best matcha that I've had in Toronto -- very strong matcha taste, fresh strawberry jam, and not too sweet.

They have amazing-looking desserts here, so I'm definitely coming back sometime.

2. **10 DEAN** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/d6Aog3esZuvxS7cq9), [Website](https://www.10dean.ca/s/order), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/10deancafe/?hl=en))

Strawberry matcha latte: **$8.00** before tax. Amazing strawberry and matcha flavour -- they use syrup and not jam here, so I liked that none of the strawberry bits could get stuck in my straw. It was VERY sweet though, so I wish the syrup could just be a bit less sweet.

Getting a drink comes with 2 hours of studying time there, and the decorations are BEAUTIFUL. Very nice study spot to take a break from UofT libraries.

3. **ICHA TEA** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HDW4DSXTFwWYibXEA), [Website](https://ichateashop.com/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ichateaca/))

Strawberry mango matcha latte: **$7.20** before tax. Very good matcha, I honestly didn't like the mango that much though? There were too many fruit bits that were hard to drink with a straw. Nice atmosphere and I want to try the tea bar!

Their specialty is more in Chinese teas, so I am definitely going back to try more. I did try the snowy rose jasmine milk tea, and it was okay, but I think tapioca pearls/toppings aren't their strongest selling point.

4. **Bloom Cafe** ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3xiFWEcfirQAMM1bA), [Website](https://thebloomcafe.ca/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bloom.caf/))

Kinako matcha latte: somehow the price is not available online, and I had it a while ago. :( It was very nicely not-too-sweet and had a pretty strong matcha taste. I'd like to go back and try their desserts!

**Conclusion:** matcha is really expensive... and let's not worry about how much money I spent on food this year...
